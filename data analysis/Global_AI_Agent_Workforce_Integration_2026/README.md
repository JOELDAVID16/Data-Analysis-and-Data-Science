# AI Agent Workforce Integration: SQL Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

As part of **My Sql Learning**, I explored a dataset about AI agent adoption in the global workforce using SQL.

The goal was to investigate how organizations are integrating AI agents into different industries, their impact on productivity, employee sentiment, deployment costs, and the risks associated with AI adoption.

Instead of focusing only on basic statistics, I approached the dataset from a business analysis perspective to identify useful patterns and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze AI agent adoption across industries.
* Investigate productivity improvements associated with AI agents.
* Identify the most common AI agent roles.
* Understand employee sentiment toward AI integration.
* Compare AI deployment costs across industries and roles.
* Examine AI governance practices and cybersecurity incidents.
* Investigate the relationship between productivity, cost, and risk.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **SQL** — Data exploration and business analysis
* **SQLiteOnline** — SQL query execution
* **Dataset:** Global AI Agent Workforce Integration 2026

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. AI Adoption Across Industries

Examined how AI agents are being integrated into different industries.

### 2. Productivity Impact

Investigated productivity-related metrics to understand the reported impact of AI agents on workforce efficiency.

### 3. AI Agent Roles

Analyzed the primary roles assigned to AI agents across organizations.

### 4. Employee Sentiment

Explored employee attitudes toward AI agent integration in the workplace.

### 5. Deployment Costs

Compared AI deployment costs across industries and agent roles to understand differences in implementation expenses.

### 6. AI Governance & Cybersecurity

Investigated governance strictness and cybersecurity incidents to understand the risks associated with AI adoption.

### 7. Business Trade-offs

Compared productivity impact, deployment costs, and cybersecurity risks across industries to explore the balance between business value and operational risk.
